We use the collected data (specifically, the date of birth) solely for: generating a personalized response (numerological calculation) using neural networks (Google Gemini); providing feedback via Instagram Direct messages.

If you would like us to delete your entire chat history with the bot and your Instagram ID from our technical database, please write to us via direct messages at our account: @ai_numerologist.
